# Budgets in GCP
In Google Cloud Platform (GCP), budgets are spending limits you set on a billing account or project to track and control cloud costs. They work with alerts that notify you when your spend approaches or exceeds thresholds, helping prevent surprise bills.

## Overview

1. Budgets in GCP are part of the Billing and Cost Management system. 

2. They allow you to:
- Define a monthly, quarterly, or annual spending limit.
- Monitor usage against that budget.
- Trigger alerts when spending reaches a percentage of the budget (e.g., 50%, 90%, 100%).
Budgets don’t stop usage automatically — they notify you so you can take action.

## Key Features

- Scope: Budgets can be applied at the billing account, project, or folder level.

- Alerts: Configurable thresholds (e.g., 50%, 90%, 100%) send email or Pub/Sub notifications.

- Forecasting: GCP can forecast spend based on historical usage and alert if projected costs exceed the budget.

## Steps to Set Budget

1. Login in to console.cloud.google.com . Now in Project picker Select organization.

2. Now Click on Navigational Menu. Click on Billing. Select My Billing.
